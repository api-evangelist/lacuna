# Lacuna

> Lacuna is an AI music creation platform that turns lyrics or plain text into complete songs with vocals — chat with a music agent or use a hands-on custom form, no musical training required. Sign up to start with free monthly credits.

Lacuna (lacuna.fm) is operated by JOYLINK LTD. The platform offers AI music generation — through a conversational music agent or a direct custom form, with a choice of song models — plus long-form radio for focus, sleep, relaxation, nature ambience, and sound masking; lyrics writing; word-level timed lyrics; lyric video export; mastering; stem separation; album-cover art; MIDI tools; and sheet-music conversion for musicians, songwriters, and content creators. The interface is available in 9 languages (en, zh-CN, zh-TW, ja, ko, es, fr, de, pt). Sign up to get free monthly credits; paid plans add more credits, priority processing, and a commercial license with full copyright to your generated tracks.

## Music & Lyrics

- [Music Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-song-generator): Generate full songs with vocals from lyrics or a text prompt. Work in Agent mode (chat with a music assistant) or Custom mode (a direct form for style, lyrics, title, vocals, and model); choose from multiple song models.
- [Lyrics Writer](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-lyrics-generator): AI-assisted lyrics writing workspace with structure, rhyme, and stylistic guidance.
- [Stem Splitter](https://www.lacuna.fm/stem-splitter): AI stem splitter that separates any song into isolated vocals, drums, bass, guitar, and piano tracks — for karaoke, remixing, and instrument learning.
- [Album Cover Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/album-cover-generator): Generate AI album covers from a text prompt or a reference image; multiple aspect ratios and instant results.

## Song Generators by Genre & Occasion

- [AI Country Song Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-country-song-generator): Turn a story, place, or feeling into a full country song with lead vocals and a real band — pedal steel, fiddle, and banjo included; write lyrics with AI or keep it instrumental.
- [AI Rap Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-rap-generator): Generate rap tracks with AI-written or your own bars over a chosen beat.
- [AI Diss Track Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-diss-track-generator): Name a target, describe your relationship, and supply the material; pick the heat level (playful, spicy, savage), a clean-language switch, and one of six beats.
- [AI Birthday Song Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-birthday-song-generator): A personalised birthday song with the right name, real details, and in-jokes.
- [AI Jingle Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-jingle-generator): Advertising jingles from a brand name and tagline — the tagline is sung word for word as the closing hook line; choose a sting, a classic spot, or a full jingle.
- [8-Bit Music Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/8-bit-music-generator): Chiptune and retro game music. Pick the scene (boss battle, overworld, title screen, victory fanfare, dungeon crawl, speed run), the console voice (NES, Game Boy, 16-bit, arcade FM), tempo, and a loop-friendly mode with no intro or outro.

## Lyrics, Video & Audio Tools

- [LRC Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/lrc-generator): Upload a song and get its lyrics back with a timestamp on every word — paste your own lyrics to align them, or leave it empty to transcribe first. Hand-editable timings, translation into 10 languages, and export as LRC, word-by-word LRC, SRT, VTT, plain text, or bilingual LRC.
- [Lyric Video Maker](https://www.lacuna.fm/lyric-video-maker): Turn a track and its timed lyrics into a lyric video. Five looks with per-effect control (rain, snow, smoke, lightning, disco beams, blur, cover zoom or pan, film grain), beat-synced motion, real-time preview, and export in 9:16, 1:1, or 16:9 at 720p or 1080p.
- [AI Mastering](https://www.lacuna.fm/ai-mastering): Master a track against a reference song or a preset; matches loudness, tonal balance, and dynamics, with gapless A/B comparison and integrated LUFS, true peak, and dynamic range on every master.
- [Mashup Maker](https://www.lacuna.fm/mashup-maker): Put one song's vocal over another song's instrumental, with tempo, key, and bar lines matched automatically. Pairs that genuinely don't fit are refused and refunded instead of rendered badly; re-balancing levels or nudging the timing re-renders for free.
- [Song Name Generator](https://www.lacuna.fm/song-name-generator): Generate song title ideas from a theme, mood, or genre. No sign-in required.
- [MP3 Tag Editor](https://www.lacuna.fm/mp3-tag-editor): Edit MP3 ID3 tags — title, artist, album, cover art — entirely in the browser; files never leave your device.
